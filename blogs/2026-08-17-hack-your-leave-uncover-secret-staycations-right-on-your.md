While the Southern Hemisphere transitions into the warmth of early summer and the Northern Hemisphere edges into the crisp tones of autumn, September can be the perfect time of year to get away, if only for a couple of days. Smart travelers are bypassing logistics-heavy itineraries in favor of effortless exploration on their doorstep. 72%* of global travelers state they would book an overnight or short stay close to home, what many would know as a staycation - broadly defined by minimal transit time (under two hours by car) and maximal relaxation.
