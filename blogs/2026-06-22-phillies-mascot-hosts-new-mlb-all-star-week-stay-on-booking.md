NEW YORK, June 22, 2026 — Booking.com, the Official Online Travel Partner of Major League Baseball, today announced The Phanatic's Phantastic Pad , a one-of-a-kind stay launching for MLB All-Star Week in Philadelphia. For the first time ever, the Phillie Phanatic – a 6’ 6” flightless green mascot with more followers than the players he heckles – is opening a Phanatic-themed two-bedroom apartment in the heart of the city to one lucky booker and their guest. Booking.com research shows that 77% of travelers see attending a major sporting event as a once-in-a-lifetime experience, and this stay is designed to make a trip to this year’s MLB All-Star Week in Philadelphia unforgettable.
