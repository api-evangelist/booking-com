The September equinox (this year falling on September 23) is more than a seasonal turning point. For centuries, cultures around the world have marked the equinox as a moment of balance, renewal and spiritual reflection, while astrologers view it as a significant shift in the cosmic calendar that signals the start of a new seasonal cycle. While the Northern Hemisphere welcomes the arrival of fall, the Southern Hemisphere steps into spring.
