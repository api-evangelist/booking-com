Today Booking.com released new research showing that Americans who regularly travel see it as an investment in their quality of life and believe the cost is worth it, even when financial sacrifice is required. The survey finds that many U.S travelers are willing to cut back elsewhere from their budget or postpone other purchases to travel. While some spend more than planned and even avoid checking their bank account or credit card balance when they get home, 88% say that when they return from a trip, the expenses feel worth it.
