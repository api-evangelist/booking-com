Ahead of Easter, Booking.com is inviting travellers to trade crowds for calm seas with the Booking.com Seafood Sail . The exclusive two-night stay gives bookers VIP access to the bustling new Sydney Fish Market, to then enjoy a seafood feast aboard a classic yacht on Sydney Harbour, led by Australian culinary icon Peter Gilmore. Booking.com’s Taste of Home Report * highlights how culinary travel is shaping the way Aussies stay in homes and alternative accommodations.
