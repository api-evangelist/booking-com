Across the world, some of the most memorable trips are defined not just by where you go, but by where you stay. With 89%* of travelers expressing interest in staying in alternative accommodation types beyond just hotels or resorts, demand is growing for stays that feel more rooted in local culture and place. From centuries-old ryokans in Japan to carved cave hotels set within the heart of a UNESCO World Heritage Site in Italy and overwater luxury villas in the Maldives, these properties turn a night’s sleep into a cultural experience that is unique in its own right.
