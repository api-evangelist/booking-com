While exploring the world brings immense joy, recent data from Booking.com’s 2026 Travel Proud Research Report reveals that the reality for LGBTQ+ travelers is often complex. Strikingly, only 31% of LGBTQ+ travelers globally report being ‘out’ when they travel, with 40% admitting they are willing to conceal their identity to experience a bucket-list destination. To mark five years of the Travel Proud program, Booking.com highlights destinations around the world with a range of Travel Proud properties where all travelers are welcome.
